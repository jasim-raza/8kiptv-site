Questions

Everything you might ask

How does the free trial work?

New viewers get 24 hours of full access at no cost. There is no payment required and no automatic charge when the trial ends — you simply decide afterwards whether to subscribe.

What internet speed do I need?

Around 10 Mbps is enough for HD, 25 Mbps for 4K, and 50 Mbps or more for 8K. The player adapts automatically, so a slower connection will simply drop to a lower quality rather than stop.

How many devices can I use at once?

One, two, three, or five simultaneous streams depending on the plan you choose. You can install the app on as many devices as you like — the limit applies only to how many play at the same time.

How quickly is my account activated?

Activation is automatic. Credentials are usually delivered within a few minutes of confirmation, and never later than one hour.

What if a channel buffers?

Try switching to an alternative server in the app settings. If the issue continues, contact support and we will move your account to a different region.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. There is no contract and no cancellation fee. Your access simply runs to the end of the period you have already paid for.

Do you offer a refund?

A 30-day money-back guarantee applies to first-time subscriptions. If the service does not work for you, contact support within 30 days for a full refund.

Is 8K IPTV a real service?

Yes — 8K IPTV is a premium streaming service that delivers live TV channels, movies, and series over the internet in ultra-high-definition quality, including HD, FHD, 4K, and 8K.

Where does the artwork come from?

Every illustration, poster and icon is original SVG drawn directly in code. Channel logos are sourced from the public tv-logos repository and are used for demonstration purposes only.
